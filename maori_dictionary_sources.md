# Māori Dictionary App — Source Overview

| Source | URL | Status | Use in App |
|---|---|---|---|
| Williams Dictionary (NZETC) | https://nzetc.victoria.ac.nz/tm/scholarly/tei-WillDict.html | CC BY-SA, freely usable now | Primary offline corpus |
| Internet Archive (Williams) | https://archive.org/details/dictionaryofmaor0000herb | Free download available | Backup copy of Williams |
| POLLEX | https://pollex.eva.mpg.de | Contact required, no clear licence | Etymology layer |
| Paekupu | https://paekupu.co.nz | Contact MoE | Curriculum/educational layer |
| Te Aka | https://maoridictionary.co.nz | Contact required | Modern Māori definitions |
| He Pātaka Kupu | https://hepatakakupu.nz | Contact Te Taura Whiri | Monolingual Māori layer |
| Personal lexicon | — | Fully in your control | User's own word list |
| PDF wordlist | — | Fully in your control | Additional vocabulary layer |

## Notes

- **Williams Dictionary** is the only source with a confirmed open licence (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 NZ). It can be bundled for offline use immediately, with attribution.
- **POLLEX** is an etymological/comparative resource, not a definition-first dictionary. Best used as a supplementary layer showing cognates across Polynesian languages.
- **Paekupu**, **Te Aka**, and **He Pātaka Kupu** all require direct contact before use. The offline rural Northland use case is a strong pitch for all three.
- The **Internet Archive** copy of Williams is useful as a bulk download if the NZETC site proves difficult to scrape structurally.
