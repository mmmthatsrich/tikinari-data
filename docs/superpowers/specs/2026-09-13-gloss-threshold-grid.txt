Two things to read off the table.

The 'concepts' column is 95,116 on every row. The grouping is identical at all
25 points, so the four recorded answers cannot discriminate between them and
the selection is on the TIER, never on which words are which.

The ceiling-5 column is the only one that fails, at every floor: himoemoe's
te_matatiki membership rests on one gloss_overlap at coverage 0.20 with df 7
(the word 'acidic', in exactly 7 glosses corpus-wide), so a ceiling below 7
tiers that concept uncertain and the export withholds it (himo^ = 0).

Stepping the ceiling one at a time at floor 0.5, off-grid, puts the cliff
between 6 and 7 rather than at 10:

    ceil   himo^   ships
       5       0  89,311
       6       0  89,569
       7       4  89,803   <- the cliff
      10       4  90,418   <- SET, three steps of deliberate clearance

10 rather than 7 because the pin is a single pair's df and would move under
ordinary corpus drift — one new source glossing something 'acidic' is enough —
and it would move silently. 615 concepts of tightness given up knowingly; see
the comment above the constants in scripts/concept_evidence.py.

The floor has no such constraint: every floor here satisfies every criterion,
which is why the section 2 cap of 0.5 is applied at the end and why the two
selection lines differ. What is SET in concept_evidence.py is the second one.
